Who is responsible

What we keep, why, and on what basis (GDPR)

  • Account: name, address, language, and your recovery address if you add one (with the date it was confirmed) — to provide the service (contract).
  • Password recovery: when a link or confirmation code is asked for, the address it was sent to and the network address (IP) — kept 30 days, to protect accounts (legitimate interest). Codes and links are stored only in a form that cannot be read back.
  • Your messages, contacts and settings — to provide the service (contract). We do not read them, except at your request or when the law requires it.
  • Security: network address (IP) and browser at sign-up and sign-ins, kept 180 days — to protect accounts (legitimate interest).
  • Orders and invoices: what you bought, amounts, owner details of a domain — kept 10 years as accounting law requires (legal obligation).
  • Copy of an old mailbox: the old provider’s password is encrypted and erased as soon as the copy ends.

Who receives data

  • Stripe (card payments) — card details go directly to Stripe.
  • The domain registrar OpenSRS (Tucows Inc., Canada) and the registry (AFNIC, EURid, …) for domain names; registries publish some owner details in the WHOIS directory unless privacy applies.
  • Our hosting providers ([host]).
  • No advertising, no tracking, no sale or sharing for marketing.

Transfers outside the EU

Railway Corporation (United States), during the launch phase only: EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. OpenSRS / Tucows (Canada): adequacy decision of the European Commission. Stripe: EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

How long

As long as your account exists. When you close it, your mailbox, mail, own domains and settings are deleted 14 days after your request (you can cancel until then); invoices are kept 10 years and security records of sign-ins 180 days, as the law and account protection require. Backups that still contain them are overwritten within 30 days.

Your rights

You can download your data yourself (Settings → Security → Your data) and close your account there. You can also ask to access, correct, delete or receive your data, object to or limit its processing: write to [email]. You can also complain to the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).
