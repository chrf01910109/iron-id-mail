1. What is sold

  • Own domain set-up — 2 € incl. VAT (the price shown when you order), paid once: IRON ID prepares your domain for email (records to publish, checks, signing keys) and copies your old emails from your previous provider (up to 5 copies).

The service starts as soon as the payment is confirmed. For a domain you own, it is ready once you have published the records shown and we have verified them. A domain name is registered within minutes, sometimes more for some registries.

As a consumer you normally have 14 days to withdraw. By asking, when you order, for the service to start right away, you accept that you lose this right once the service has been fully provided (art. L221-28 of the French Consumer Code) — in particular once a domain name has been registered. If the service has not started yet, write to [email] within 14 days of the order for a full refund.

5. Domain names

You are the owner (registrant) of the domain. You can move it to another registrar; for .com, .net and .org the ICANN rules allow this 60 days after registration or after a previous transfer, and for .fr and .eu the AFNIC and EURid rules apply. We give you the transfer code on request. Renewal is not automatic: you pay each year from 30 days before the end of the year paid; without renewal the domain expires and may be taken by someone else. The registries’ rules apply (AFNIC for .fr, EURid for .eu, ICANN for .com/.net/.org).

7. Complaints and mediation

Contact [email] first. If no solution is found, you can refer the matter free of charge to the consumer mediator [name, website] or to the European Online Dispute Resolution platform.

8. Law

French law applies. As a consumer you may go to the court of your place of residence or to any court competent under the law; for professional customers, the courts of [city of the registered office] are competent.
