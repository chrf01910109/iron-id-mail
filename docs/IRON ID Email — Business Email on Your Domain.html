What is IRON ID Email?

Business email for your organization, on your domain.

Email gives every member a professional mailbox and every team a shared one, on the IRON ID domain or on your own. It is part of the same organization as Workspace and Storage: same accounts, same MFA, same roles.

What makes it different is who a message comes from. Between IRON ID organizations, the sender is a verified identity, and an attachment sent from Storage keeps its fingerprint.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask first.

What is a standard mailbox?

A mailbox on the IRON ID domain, included with every organization. A mailbox on your own domain is a paid option.

Can we keep our existing domain?

Yes. Connect your domain and create mailboxes on it.

Do recipients outside IRON ID need anything?

No. They receive a standard email. The verified sender badge appears between IRON ID organizations.

When is it available?

Email is in early access, being built on the shared trust layer. Join early access to test it first.
